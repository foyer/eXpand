# eXpand privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

eXpand is a browser extension that changes how x.com (formerly twitter.com) is displayed. It runs entirely inside your browser.

## What it accesses

- **Pages on x.com and twitter.com.** The extension restyles these pages and reads their content to do so. It does not run on any other site.
- **X's own API responses.** While you browse, X's web app loads timeline data from X's servers. eXpand reads those responses inside the page to learn the full length of long posts, so it can decide whether to expand them. This happens in memory, on your device.
- **Your settings.** Column width, media sizes, theme and hide/show choices are saved with the browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own devices through your browser account.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit any data about you, your account, your posts or your browsing.
- It does not contact any server. The extension has no backend and makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not include analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It does not load or run code from outside the extension package.

## Permissions

- `storage`: to save your settings.
- Host access to `x.com` and `twitter.com`: to run on X's pages.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension and the date above will be updated.

## Contact

Questions can be raised through the extension's listing page or its source repository.
