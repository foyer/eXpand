# Changelog

## 0.9.0 (beta) — 2026-10-02

First public beta.

- Wider timeline column (default 1200px, 600–1600px, or fill the window) with the left nav collapsed to an icon rail and the right sidebar hidden or trimmed to search.
- Auto-expands “Show more” on long posts up to a configurable rendered height (default 15 lines).
- Enlarges single photos up to a configurable height (default 800px) without cropping; lifts X's 510px cap.
- Enlarges portrait videos, optionally cropped to 3:4, 4:5 or square, with a per-video toggle and full-frame fullscreen.
- Classic Dim theme over X's Lights out.
- Declutter options: promoted posts (hidden by default), Home composer, feed tab bar, view counts, engagement counts, Grok button, and individual left-nav items.
- Layout changes are applied before X measures post heights, so scroll position is kept when going back from a post.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-28

Initial version: wide column, icon nav, hidden sidebar, auto-expand by character count.
